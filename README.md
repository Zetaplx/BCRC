# Bitcraft Remote Compendium
Welcome to the Bitcraft Remote Compendium (or the BCRC), a tool designed to be your right hand as you explore the world of Starfell with your friends.
Our goal is to provide remote access to a complete compendium of knowledge, like what you can find in game and more. While we are still very early in development, we have big plans to develope a number of tools to help you on your journey.

## Planned Features
### Compendium Import
Everything you can find in the in game compendium as well as additional information otherwise unlisted. Figure out what it takes to craft that legendary axe and you odds of success. Find exactly where those T4 berries are hiding from you. 

### Interactive Map
An interactive display of the games many regions including including key locations. Use your componedium to select resources to highlight where they can be found on the map! Mark key settlements and trade routes with our annotation tools.

### Explorers Welcome
Exploration is a key fascet of the game, and we don't want to take away from that. By default, most systems will be fairly empty, showing all Tier 1 information. You can fill in higher tier information as you travel, plotting what you see and where. Adding to your map and compendium as you go.

## Pre-Development
This project is in its earliest of stages, what we are terming pre-development. Our goals may shift and the details will certainly change as time goes on. If you have any feedback or suggestions at any point in this process feel free to note them here! Best of luck on your adventures!
